01 / PHILOSOPHY

Wabi-Sabi & Kintsugi

This project is inspired by the traditional Japanese aesthetics of finding beauty in imperfection, impermanence, and simplicity. Through kintsugi, broken pottery is repaired with gold lacquer—treating breakage and repair as part of the object's history, rather than something to disguise.

02 / EXPERIMENT

Digital Fracture

Fragments are procedurally generated and suspended in an anti-gravity field. By dragging, rotating, and scaling the ceramic shards, you participate in the restoration process. Once released, the shards reassemble, sealing the seams with gold leaf underlays and ambient soundscapes.

03 / THE MAKER

Sole Creator

Conceptualized, designed, and developed entirely by a single creator. Focused on merging interactive physics, generative aesthetics, and meditative soundscapes into a tactile browser experience.
