About Imperfect Vessel

An interactive digital space exploring perfectionism, cubism, and the art of letting go.

“
“I built this project because I spent too much time believing every flaw needed fixing.”

Perfectionism rarely feels like confidence. More often, it feels like fear—a quiet worry that one mistake might ruin everything. Imperfect Vessel is designed to unlearn that fear through tactile disruption and reconstruction.

01PURPOSE

What if nothing is actually broken?

Instead of explaining perfectionism with abstract words, this project invites you to directly experience it by shattering, rearranging, and repairing a digital vessel. Here, breaking is not the destruction of form, but the raw inception of a story.

02PHILOSOPHY

Artistic & Conceptual Lineage

Three foundational traditions shaping this digital canvas.

TRADITION 01

Wabi-Sabi & Kintsugi

In Japanese aesthetics, fractured pottery is mended with lacquer dusted with real gold—honoring cracks as irreversible history rather than flaws to hide.

TRADITION 02

Picasso’s Cubism

Picasso demonstrated that human truth defies single-point perspective. Fracturing facial planes reveals the unfiltered emotional landscape underneath.

POETIC ECHO

Leonard Cohen’s Light

“There is a crack in everything. That's how the light gets in.”

Fractures are not defects to disguise, but the exact fault lines through which illumination enters.
